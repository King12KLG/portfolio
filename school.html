From the halls of Crawford College Sandton to the lawns of Wits University, five years of school and two years of university have shaped the creative I am becoming.

January 2019 — December 2023

Crawford College
Sandton

Crawford is where curiosity became a habit. It was here that I discovered a love for storytelling, design thinking, and the kind of learning that happens outside the classroom. The foundation for everything I do now was laid in those five years.

February 2024 — Present

University of the
Witwatersrand

Studying towards a Bachelor of Arts in Digital Arts at Wits has pushed me to think critically and create with intention. In two years I have learned to code, write, design and build games and picked up animation skills along the way. This degree sits exactly at the intersection of technology and art, which is where I have always belonged.

Johannesburg raised me.
Both institutions shaped me.
