Champions of Tennis

Champions of Tennis is a Unity-built tennis game inspired by the mechanics and rhythm of real tennis matches. Players compete across sets, serving and rallying against an opponent. Born from my obsession with the sport and my curiosity about what it feels like to play something you love inside a screen.

Arcade · Unity

Lumberjack Gold

Lumberjack Gold is an arcade-style game that takes inspiration from old-school gameplay mechanics. Players chop, dodge and score in a fast-paced environment. This project reflects my interest in reconfiguring classic arcade experiences with a fresh visual approach.

Arcade · Unity

Classic Pong

A faithful recreation of the original Pong arcade game, rebuilt in Unity. This was an exercise in understanding the fundamentals of game physics, collision detection, and score systems. Sometimes going back to the beginning is the most instructive thing a developer can do.
