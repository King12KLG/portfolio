About

Motheo
Ngwetjana

I am a 21-year-old Digital Arts student at the University of the Witwatersrand, Johannesburg. My work lives at the intersection of writing, game development, and photography shaped by a deep love for culture and the city I grew up in.

The Writer

I write about the things that move me art, Wits, film, music, and the streets of Johannesburg. Writing is how I make sense of the world. From essays to cultural reviews, every article is an attempt to find meaning in the details others overlook.

The Developer

I build games. Not because I have to, but because I am fascinated by the way interactivity creates experience. My tennis game, Champions of Tennis, was born from a simple question what does it feel like to play a sport you love, inside a screen?

The Photographer

I photograph life. The nature trails of Wits campus, the streets of Sophiatown, the light in Limpopo. Photography taught me to slow down and look. It is the most honest thing I do.
